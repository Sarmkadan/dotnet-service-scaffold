# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all people who want to participate. Our community is based on mutual respect, tolerance, and understanding. We do not tolerate harassment or bullying of any community member in any form.

This Code of Conduct applies to all community interactions: GitHub repositories, issue trackers, discussions, pull requests, social media, and in-person events.

## Expected Behavior

All participants in our community are expected to:

### Be Respectful
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community

### Be Collaborative
- Work together constructively
- Support other community members
- Share knowledge and experience
- Help others succeed

### Be Professional
- Maintain professional communication
- Respect intellectual property
- Give proper attribution to others' work
- Report issues appropriately

### Be Inclusive
- Welcome people of all backgrounds
- Avoid discriminatory language
- Create an environment where all feel valued
- Include diverse perspectives in decision-making

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment and bullying include, but are not limited to:

- Violent threats or language directed toward others
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Posting sexually explicit or violent material
- Posting others' private information without consent
- Personal insults or attacks
- Repeated harassment despite requests to stop
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above behaviors
- Trolling, flaming, baiting, or other disruptive behavior

## Consequences of Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior will not be tolerated. Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately.

If a community member engages in unacceptable behavior, the community organizers may take action, including:
- A warning
- Temporary suspension from community interactions
- Permanent removal from the community
- Blocking of accounts and repositories

## Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it by:

1. **GitHub Issues**: Report through the GitHub issue tracker
2. **Email**: Contact rutova2@gmail.com with details
3. **Pull Request**: Comment on the relevant PR

When reporting, include:
- Description of the incident
- Who was involved
- When it occurred
- Any relevant context or evidence
- Any steps already taken

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and confidentially.

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. They will:
- Review and investigate all reports impartially
- Maintain confidentiality of reporters
- Take appropriate action based on findings
- Communicate decisions clearly

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All GitHub repositories and organizations
- Project discussions and issue trackers
- Pull request comments and reviews
- Project documentation
- Any spaces designated as community spaces
- Representation of the project or community at events
